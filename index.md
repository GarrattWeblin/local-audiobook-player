# Earmark

A calm, offline audiobook player for Android, for the DRM-free audiobooks you already have on your phone (M4B, M4A/AAC and MP3). It has chapters, bookmarks, a sleep timer, backup and restore, and Android Auto. There's no account, no ads and no internet access.
