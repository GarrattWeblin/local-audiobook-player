# Privacy policy: Earmark

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

Earmark ("the app") is published by Garratt Weblin. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **nothing leaves your device unless you move it yourself.**

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. It does not request internet access: the Android permission that allows network use is not in the app, so it cannot send data anywhere.

## What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

- the list of audiobook folders you chose, and the books it found in them (titles, authors, chapters, cover images taken from the files);
- your listening position, speed, bookmarks and notes;
- a log of when and how long you listened, used for listening stats and goals;
- your settings;
- if you subscribe to Plus, your subscription status (see below).

Your audio files are only read, never changed, copied or uploaded.

## Earmark Plus (optional subscription)

Plus is an optional monthly or yearly subscription sold through **Google Play**.

- **Payments are handled by Google Play**, under the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app never sees your card or payment details, and the developer does not receive your name or email address from your purchase.
- The app asks the Google Play app on your phone whether you have an active subscription. It checks Google's signature on the answer on your phone and stores only the result (your plan and how long it is valid) in its private storage. This status is not included in backups.
- You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or with **Manage subscription** in the app.
- If Plus ends, nothing you created is deleted: your notes, listening history and settings stay on your device.

## Permissions

- **Access to audio files / chosen folders**: to find and play the audiobooks you choose. Folder access is granted through Android's folder picker and can be removed in the app (Settings → Library folders) or in Android settings.
- **Notifications**: to show playback controls while a book is playing, and, if you turn it on, a daily listening-goal reminder.
- **Foreground service and wake lock**: to keep playing with the screen off.
- **Google Play billing**: to check and buy the optional Plus subscription through the Google Play app.

## Backups and sharing

- **Backup file.** If you use _Settings → Back up progress and bookmarks_, the app writes a file to a location you pick. It contains your progress, bookmarks, notes, listening log and settings. What happens to it then (for example, if you save it to a cloud folder) is up to you and that service.
- **Sharing your yearly recap.** If you choose to share your recap, the app makes an image and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you do this.
- **Android backup.** If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's settings and its library database (book list, listening progress, bookmarks, notes and listening log) in it. That is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by the app's developer. Cover images and your subscription status are left out.

## Android Auto and widgets

When you use the app in your car, Android Auto shows your library and playback controls on the car's screen. Home-screen widgets show the book you are listening to on your phone's home screen. In both cases the information stays between the app and your phone or car display.

## Children

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
